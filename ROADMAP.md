### Offer CardHop at checkout

Done. When you click into a credit card field, CardHop can show a small prompt in the top-right corner with buttons to open your virtual cards or a saved card page. It is opt-in (Settings → At checkout), asks for all-sites access only after explaining why, and only checks whether a field is a card field. See the README for details and known gaps.
